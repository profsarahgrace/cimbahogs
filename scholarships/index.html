The big picture

Three places to look for money.

Most students apply to more than one. Each layer is run by a different office, so applying to one does not apply you to the others.

1

CIMBA awards

Open to CIMBA students who meet each award’s criteria

Merit, need-based, leadership, ambassador, and sibling awards from $100 to $1,500 for summer.

Apply: CIMBA’s online forms

See CIMBA awards ↓
2

University of Arkansas awards

Open to UA students, including Walton students

Named study abroad scholarships from the Office of Study Abroad, plus scholarships from individual colleges.

Apply: one application in the Hogs Abroad portal, open from January and reviewed on a rolling basis

See UA awards ↓
3

Honors College grant

Open to Honors College students

The Study Abroad Grant covers about 30–36% of allowable costs and can be combined with other scholarships.

Apply: the Honors Student Hub, open from early fall and reviewed on a rolling basis

See the Honors grant ↓

Not sure where to start?

  • Every student: start with the CIMBA awards and the Hogs Abroad scholarship application.
  • Honors College student: add the Study Abroad Grant. You can submit as early as the fall semester, and it is reviewed on a rolling basis.
  • Receiving financial aid at Arkansas: the CIMBA need-based award is built for you.
  • Have a sibling who attended CIMBA: ask about the Legacy Award within 30 days of acceptance.

Layer 1 · CIMBA

CIMBA scholarships for summer.

These are the summer amounts. Semester awards are larger and are listed on CIMBA’s site. You apply to CIMBA directly, separate from any University of Arkansas form.

Layer 2 · University of Arkansas

UA study abroad scholarships.

The Office of Study Abroad administers a set of named awards. Undergraduates complete one application in the Hogs Abroad portal after starting a program application.

Office of Study Abroad awards

  • Andrew Vongnarath Memorial Study Abroad Award
  • Henley Family Advance Arkansas Study Abroad Experience Endowed Scholarship
  • Kristen Collier Wright International Education and Study Abroad Endowed Scholarship
  • Nathan “Kitt” Rom Memorial Study Abroad Scholarship
  • Study Abroad Consortium for Academic Partnership Scholarship

The John and Trudy Sher International Service Learning Award is for service-learning courses only.

College-specific scholarships

Bumpers College, Education and Health Professions, Fulbright College, Engineering, the Sam M. Walton College of Business, and the Honors College each offer their own study abroad scholarships. Ask your college’s scholarship or advising office what is open to your student.

Check the Hogs Abroad portal for current amounts and deadlines. UA study abroad funding guide · Hogs Abroad portal

Layer 3 · Honors College

Honors College funding.

Honors students have their own study abroad funding on top of the layers above.

Study Abroad Grant

  • Covers roughly 30–36% of allowable costs, with extra funding for students with financial need.
  • Works for programs from two weeks to a year, and students may receive it more than once.
  • Can be combined with other scholarships, academic aid, Pell Grants, and existing financial aid.
  • You can submit as early as the fall semester, and awards are made on a rolling basis, so apply early. Apply through the Honors Student Hub.

Libby Finch Study Abroad Award. A second Honors award for study in the UK and Europe. Recipients blog monthly during their time abroad.

Honors global experiences page · Questions: honors@uark.edu or 479-575-7678
