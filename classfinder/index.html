How the schedule comes together

  1. 1
    Pre-approve six courses in DICE

    DICE pre-approves CIMBA classes for transfer credit at Arkansas. Start in January: approval can take one to twelve weeks. Choose six for backups, and follow the DICE Student Guide.

  2. 2
    Request classes with CIMBA

    Wait for DICE approval before requesting your classes. Popular courses can fill up, so your pre-approved backups matter.

  3. 3
    CIMBA confirms your two

    You'll hear back on your final schedule in Mar–Apr.

  4. 4
    Credit transfers to UA

    Two 3‑credit‑hour courses, six credit hours total, earned through the University of Iowa.
