Where the two columns meet

Upload your CIMBA acceptance letter to Hogs Abroad

Once you've received an acceptance letter from CIMBA, upload it to Hogs Abroad. This is the step that connects the two applications, and we don't want you to miss it! It's what moves your U of A status from conditionally accepted to committed.

Until you do it, Arkansas does not know you got in, and your application sits unfinished.

A few friendly heads-ups

Things that surprise people

The application grows as you go

What you see when you first open Hogs Abroad is only part of the total. New forms load as you finish earlier ones, so keep checking back even after a screen looks complete.

Keep both applications moving

It is tempting to wait and hear back from one before starting the other. Working on them side by side gives you a lot more breathing room.

Watch your email

Almost every prompt arrives by email, much of it from messages@via-trm.com. Add that address to your contacts, and make sure the address on file with Hogs Abroad is one you check often.

Give DICE plenty of time

Approval can take up to twelve weeks, and you cannot register for classes until it clears. Submitting in January keeps you comfortably ahead of it.

Pre-approve six classes on DICE

Listing backups now saves you a lot of trouble later. If a first-choice class fills and you have no pre-approved alternate, you would have to start that approval over.

Admitted and enrolled are different

Getting into CIMBA is wonderful news, and your spot is held once you submit the Intent to Enroll form. Uploading your acceptance letter then wraps up the Arkansas side.

If you are a parent helping a student stay on track

Two checkpoints are worth asking about, since both involve waiting periods that are easy to underestimate. The first is the Study Abroad 101 session, which has to be scheduled in advance and tends to fill up as deadlines get closer. The second is the DICE form in January, where approval can take up to twelve weeks and class registration waits on it.

A helpful question to ask is "which of the two applications are you further along on, and what is the next form waiting on you." Because there are two columns to finish, students sometimes feel done once one of them is complete.

The October 22 hackathon is also a great nudge. Students who come tend to leave with most of the hard parts behind them.

Questions about the Hogs Abroad side are best answered by the Study Abroad office. For anything about CIMBA itself, the courses, or life in Paderno del Grappa, reach out to any of us. We are happy to help.
