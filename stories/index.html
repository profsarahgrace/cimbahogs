Advice from past CimbaHogs

What they wish they had known.

Arkansas students shared their best tips for the next group heading to Paderno del Grappa.

Money comes back. If there is a trip you’re dying to take, take it! Say yes.

Three best things about Cimba: your own beautiful campus, new friends from UA, and new friends from other schools.

Pack your bag. Then take half of your things out and leave them at home.

Just say yes! Say yes to the weekend trip with the group you aren’t close with yet, to the mid-week walk to gelato, the conversations with locals on the train, and everything else that comes your way.

Soak it all in! It’s not very often you get to be in Europe for a summer. Some of my best college memories were made at CIMBA!

Don’t underestimate your week nights! Meet people, take small trips and enjoy the little moments before you get to your weekend.

Be intentional in building relationships with other students at CIMBA. These will be some of the most talented and special friends you make in college.

Say yes to every experience you are offered! The best memories are from the spontaneous day trips or crazy adventures you take on a whim.

You can’t even imagine how quickly the Cimba campus will feel like home… But it is 100% true.

Not everything will go as planned, but that is part of the fun and journey.

Don’t let the experience and friendships end in Italy, that’s just where it begins.

When you look back on your time in Italy, you will not regret the moments that pushed you.
